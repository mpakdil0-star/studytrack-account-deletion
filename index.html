ACCOUNT AND DATA

Delete your StudyTrack account

This page lets you permanently delete your StudyTrack account without reinstalling the Android app.

What will be deleted

Your profile, exam goals, availability, resources, plans, tasks, study sessions, practice-test results, mistake records, notification preferences and subscription access cache.

These StudyTrack account records are permanently removed when the request is completed; StudyTrack does not retain them afterwards. Google Play may separately retain payment and subscription records under Google's own policies.

Google Play subscription

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play to stop future billing.
